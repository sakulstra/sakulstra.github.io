## Building a Geodome: Part 1 🔎

For most of my life, I've been fascinated by geodesic domes like the Eden Project in Britain. Recently, I became captivated by a series of YouTube videos documenting the lives of people [living in greenhouses](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NGDr3Xrv0Ko). While residing in such a structure may never become a reality for me, building a geodesic greenhouse seems like an achievable goal.

### Why build a geodome instead of buying a conventional greenhouse?

Geodomes are far more visually appealing than standard greenhouses. Additionally, as I spend most of my day in front of a computer, constructing something with my own hands offers a refreshing change of pace and environment.
